Setting up your local models

PageSpindle is downloading its Qwen text model and English narration voice. This happens once and needs about 1.9 GB of downloads. After setup, processing works offline. No account or paid service is needed.

Your text stays on this computer. The download provider receives model-file requests, never your manuscript.

Starting setup…

Allow at least 8 GB of free disk space for the application, download and installation. Closing this window cancels the download. Existing model files are preserved if setup fails or is cancelled.
